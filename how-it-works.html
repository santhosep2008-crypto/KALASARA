WATER QUALITY

Improving Water Quality

KALASARA uses multiple treatment stages to progressively process contaminated water. The treatment architecture can address suspended particles, turbidity and other targeted contaminants depending on the selected membrane and treatment configuration.

02

SMART MONITORING

Health & Quality of Life

Water-quality monitoring allows the system operator to observe parameters such as pH, TDS, TSS, turbidity and temperature. This provides measurable information about the water before and after treatment.

03

LIVELIHOODS

Agriculture & Livelihoods

Reliable water quality is important for communities that depend on water resources for agriculture and daily activities. KALASARA is designed as a modular system that can be adapted and evaluated for different water-quality conditions.

04

ENVIRONMENT

Reducing Environmental Pressure

Water treatment can help manage contaminated water before it reaches downstream environments. KALASARA focuses on a controlled, modular treatment pathway with monitoring at key stages.

05

COMMUNITY

Stronger Communities

Access to better-managed water resources can contribute to safer and more resilient communities. KALASARA is being developed with portability, modularity and digital monitoring in mind.
