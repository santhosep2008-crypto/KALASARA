SMART WATER PURIFICATION SYSTEM

Turning Polluted Water Into Safer Water.

KALASARA is a modular water purification and monitoring system designed for polluted and mining-affected environments. The system combines pre-filtration, bio-coagulation, membrane-based separation and real-time water-quality monitoring.

THE PROBLEM

Water contamination needs smarter treatment.

Polluted and mining-affected water can contain suspended solids, turbidity, dissolved contaminants and changes in water chemistry.

01

TSS

Suspended particles and sediments can significantly affect water quality.

02

TURBIDITY

Fine particles make water cloudy and interfere with downstream treatment.

03

HEAVY METALS

Mining-affected water may contain dissolved metal contaminants requiring targeted treatment.

04

REMOTE AREAS

Conventional treatment infrastructure may be difficult to deploy and maintain in remote locations.

OUR SOLUTION

KALASARA

One system. Multiple treatment stages.

KALASARA follows a modular treatment architecture where different purification stages work together to progressively improve water quality.

Instead of depending on a single treatment mechanism, the system combines physical filtration, bio-assisted coagulation, membrane separation and digital monitoring.

PURIFICATION PROCESS

From contaminated water to monitored output.

01

Raw Water Chamber

Contaminated source water enters the KALASARA treatment system.

02

Stainless-Steel Mesh

The initial physical filtration stage captures larger suspended particles and debris.

03

Bio-Coagulation

Bio-assisted coagulation promotes the aggregation of fine suspended contaminants for easier separation.

04

Kalasara Membrane

The membrane stage provides controlled separation of remaining contaminants.

05

Final Treatment

Additional polishing can be incorporated depending on the required treatment configuration.

06

Quality Monitoring

Sensors continuously monitor important water-quality parameters.
