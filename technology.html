KALASARA • SMART WATER TECHNOLOGY

Technology Behind KALASARA

A compact, modular water purification system designed to treat contaminated water while continuously monitoring key water-quality parameters.

KALASARA CAD Architecture

The CAD model represents the internal arrangement of the major purification, sensing, control and power components inside the KALASARA system.

KALASARA Prototype — Internal Component Architecture
💧

Multi-Stage Purification

Water passes through multiple treatment stages designed to reduce suspended particles and selected contaminants before the final output.

🧪

KALASARA Membrane

The membrane stage acts as a selective treatment barrier within the purification process. Its performance is evaluated experimentally for the target water quality.

📡

Smart Monitoring

Sensors measure parameters such as pH, TDS, turbidity and temperature. The readings can be transmitted through an ESP32-based monitoring system.

☀️

Solar-Assisted Power

The prototype is designed with a solar power option to support operation in locations where reliable grid power may be limited.

Core Components

Major hardware elements integrated into the KALASARA prototype.

COMPONENT 01

Water Inlet

Receives untreated water and directs it into the purification stages.

COMPONENT 02

Pre-Filter

Provides initial removal of larger suspended particles before membrane treatment.

COMPONENT 03

Treatment Chamber

Provides the controlled chamber for the selected bio-based treatment process.

COMPONENT 04

KALASARA Membrane

Provides the primary selective filtration stage in the treatment pathway.

COMPONENT 05

Sensor Unit

Measures selected water-quality parameters for system monitoring.

COMPONENT 06

ESP32 Controller

Collects sensor readings and provides the connectivity layer for digital monitoring.

COMPONENT 07

Solar Power

Supports the system's power requirements through a solar-assisted energy source.

COMPONENT 08

Water Outlet

Delivers treated water after passing through the defined purification stages.

COMPONENT 09

Digital Dashboard

Displays sensor readings and system status through the KALASARA monitoring interface.
